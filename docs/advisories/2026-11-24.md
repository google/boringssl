# OpenSSL Advisory: November 24, 2026 (BoringSSL Not Affected)

OpenSSL have published a [security advisory](https://openssl-library.org/news/secadv/20261124.txt). Here's how it affects BoringSSL:

CVE | Summary | [Severity] in OpenSSL | Impact to BoringSSL
----|---------|-----------------------|---------------------
CVE-2026-84786 | Possible NULL Dereference in Non-SMTP DANE Clients | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork
CVE-2026-107494 | Unbounded QUIC RETIRE_CONNECTION_ID Frame Sending Queue | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork
CVE-2026-121482 | Relative CRL Distribution Point Names Rebuilt per CRL in X.509 Verification | Low | Not affected, BoringSSL removed relative CRL DPs entirely as part of addressing [CVE-2026-35189](./2026-09-29.md)
CVE-2026-84785 | Unbounded Out-of-Order Fragment Reassembly in QUIC Streams | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork

[Severity]: https://openssl-library.org/policies/general/security-policy/index.html#issue-severity
