# OpenSSL Advisory: November 17, 2026 (BoringSSL Not Affected)

OpenSSL have published a [security advisory](https://openssl-library.org/news/secadv/20261117.txt). Here's how it affects BoringSSL:

CVE | Summary | [Severity] in OpenSSL | Impact to BoringSSL
----|---------|-----------------------|---------------------
CVE-2026-35190 | Out-of-Bounds Read in TLS 1.3 Key-Share Processing with Suite B Certificate Flag | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork
CVE-2026-107493 | Use-After-Free in PEM_X509_INFO_read_bio_ex() When Appending to a Caller-Supplied Stack Fails | Low | Not affected, BoringSSL independently fixed this in [2018](https://boringssl-review.googlesource.com/c/boringssl/+/32109)
CVE-2026-124709 | Unbounded Memory Retention in QUIC Server for Rejected Streams | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork

[Severity]: https://openssl-library.org/policies/general/security-policy/index.html#issue-severity
