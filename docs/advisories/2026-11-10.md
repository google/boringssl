# OpenSSL Advisory: November 10, 2026 (BoringSSL Not Affected)

OpenSSL have published a [security advisory](https://openssl-library.org/news/secadv/20261110.txt). Here's how it affects BoringSSL:

CVE | Summary | [Severity] in OpenSSL | Impact to BoringSSL
----|---------|-----------------------|---------------------
CVE-2026-88822 | QUIC Uses Uninitialized Stack Bytes During Automated Peer Detection | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork
CVE-2026-88823 | AES-GCM-SIV AAD Forgery After Zero-length Incremental AAD Update | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork
CVE-2026-95691 | Retransmitted QUIC CRYPTO Frames Can Cause DoS or One-byte Disclosure | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork
CVE-2026-95692 | Out-of-Bounds Read and Incorrect IV Length Handling in AES-GCM-SIV | Low | Not affected, issue was introduced after fork
CVE-2026-107496 | Infinite Loop in CMS_encrypt() With an EC Group Order of 1 | Low | Not affected, impacted code was removed from BoringSSL

[Severity]: https://openssl-library.org/policies/general/security-policy/index.html#issue-severity
